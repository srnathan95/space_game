# Google Play Store Feature Graphic Specifications

## Requirements:
- **Dimensions:** 1024 x 500 pixels (exactly)
- **Format:** PNG (24-bit RGB or 32-bit PNG) or JPG
- **File Size:** Maximum 15MB
- **No Text:** Feature graphics should be mostly image-based (text gets cut off on different devices)
- **Safe Area:** Keep important content within the center 80% to avoid cropping on different devices

## Design Recommendations for Space Game:

### Color Scheme:
- Primary Background: Dark space blue/black (#000428 matches your app bar color)
- Accent Colors: Orange/Yellow for asteroids and bullets
- Text: White/Yellow for contrast
- Stars: White/light blue for starfield effect

### Visual Elements to Include:
1. **Space/Starfield Background** - Dark with stars (matches your game)
2. **Rocket Ship** - Prominent in center or left side
3. **Asteroids** - Multiple falling asteroids to show action
4. **Bullets/Trails** - Bright yellow/orange streaks
5. **Game Title** - "Space Game" (keep minimal, large, readable)
6. **Optional:** "Can you reach 21 points?" challenge text (small, subtle)

### Layout Suggestions:
- **Left Side (40%):** Game title "SPACE GAME" in large, bold font
- **Center/Right (60%):** Gameplay scene - rocket, asteroids, bullets in action
- **Background:** Starfield pattern matching your game

## Tools to Create Feature Graphic:

### Free Online Tools:
1. **Canva** (canva.com) - Has Google Play Store feature graphic templates
2. **Figma** (figma.com) - Free design tool
3. **GIMP** (gimp.org) - Free image editor
4. **Photopea** (photopea.com) - Browser-based Photoshop alternative

### Quick Steps:
1. Open Canva or your chosen tool
2. Create custom size: 1024 x 500 pixels
3. Use dark blue/black gradient background (#000428 or darker)
4. Add starfield pattern (scatter small white dots)
5. Add simplified rocket graphic (triangle shape, white/orange)
6. Add asteroid shapes (grey circles) at various sizes
7. Add bullet streaks (yellow/orange lines)
8. Add "SPACE GAME" title text in large, bold white font
9. Export as PNG (24-bit RGB)

## Text-Free Alternative:
Since text gets cropped, consider making it completely visual:
- Just gameplay elements (rocket, asteroids, bullets, starfield)
- No text at all - let the visuals speak
- The app name appears below in Play Store UI anyway

## Alternative Approach:
Create a screenshot from your actual game and enhance it:
1. Take a screenshot during gameplay
2. Resize/crop to 1024x500
3. Enhance colors and contrast
4. Add subtle "Space Game" text overlay
